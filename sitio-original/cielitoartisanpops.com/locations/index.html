Our shops are a true representation of who we are. From the moment you approach, you’ll be surrounded by greenery, nature, colors and wood. It’s a fresh, natural look that speaks to the ingredients you’re about to discover once you step inside!

Located in the center of the Wynwood Arts District, our First shop was opened in August, 2017. It is located in the iconic WYNWOOD BUILDING

Wynwood is an eclectic district in the urban core of Miami, Florida. It is home to art galleries, retail stores, antique shops, eclectic bars, artisanal eateries and one of the largest open-air street-art installations in the world. Artists from around the world have sought inspiration in the area’s windowless facades and used them as canvases to showcase their work, leading to the vivid murals that adorn the district.

Our Boca location opened in July 2020, in the middle of the pandemic, what a challenge! It is located at the beautiful Mizner Park.

With breathtaking fountains, inviting gazebos, shops, dining options, the Boca Raton Museum of Art, and an amphitheater (that hosts major concerts and community events), Mizner Park remains a popular destination for locals and visitors alike. Mizner Park opened in 1989 and has become the city’s meeting place.

We opened at Aventura Mall in July, 2021.

Aventura Mall is the premier shopping destination in Miami and South Florida and one of the top shopping centers in the U.S. 

Centrally located between Miami and Fort Lauderdale, the mall draws from a 30+ mile radius encompassing South Florida’s most affluent communities and is a must-see for millions of qualified domestic and international visitors.
