Made daily in our Wynwood back kitchen

We opened Cielito in 2017 to share our passion for healthy, creative and tasty sweets with our community. Every pop is hand-crafted daily at our Wynwood store.

A family-friendly ambiance lends to the fun and artistic feel, with indoor and outdoor seating. It's also perfect for pet owners: we make dog-friendly pops too!
