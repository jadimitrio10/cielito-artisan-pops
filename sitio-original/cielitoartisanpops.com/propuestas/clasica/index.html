A piece of heaven

Not your conventional ice cream shop.

Cielito offers hand-crafted, high-end frozen treats that might have the shape of a popsicle, but they are something else. We make these pops by hand, in small batches, using locally sourced ingredients — and hope you enjoy them to the last drop.

We opened Cielito in 2017 so that we could share our passion for healthy, creative and tasty sweets with our community. Each pop is hand-crafted daily in our Wynwood store's back kitchen!

A family-friendly ambiance lends to the fun and artistic feel, with indoor and outdoor seating. It's also perfect for pet owners — dog-friendly pops are offered!
