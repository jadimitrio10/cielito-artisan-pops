Simply fresh and delicious.

We opened Cielito in 2017 to share our passion for healthy, creative and tasty sweets with our community. Every pop is hand-crafted daily in our Wynwood back kitchen.

With more than 46 flavors every week, the menu changes with the seasons. You'll always find today's selection in our online shop.

Fruit & sorbet

Hand-picked fresh fruit, the best Florida has to offer.

Ice cream

Fresh milk, cream, egg yolks and cane sugar. Our family custard, pasteurized in house.

Vegan

An oat and cashew milk base, made completely in house.

Doggy pops

Fresh fruit sweetened with honey, for your pup.

We make everything from scratch.

Our pastry team makes the pastries, ice cream add-ins, fillings and jams in our Wynwood back kitchen. At Cielito we're approaching sweets in a new way, and we invite you to join us.
