How we started

Our pastry team makes the add-ins, fillings and jams in the Wynwood back kitchen. You can watch them through the big window.

A family business

Sindy grew up in Colombia in a family of bakers, and her background is in architecture. She opened the first Cielito in Wynwood in 2017 and runs it with her husband Ivan and her parents, using recipes the family has made for decades.

“Thank you for trying our treats and helping our dream come true.” Sindy and Ivan
