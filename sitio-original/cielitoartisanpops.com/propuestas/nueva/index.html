Everything is made from scratch, in our own kitchen

Our pastry team makes the ice cream bases, add-ins, fillings and jams in the back kitchen of the Wynwood shop. You can watch them work through the big window.

“We are a family run business, and we thank you for trying our treats and helping our dream come true.”
Sindy & Ivan, owners

Sindy grew up in Colombia in a family of bakers, and her background is in architecture. She opened the first Cielito in Wynwood in 2017 and runs it with her husband Ivan and her parents, using recipes the family has made for decades.
