The Craft

Made by hand.
Every single day.

  • Locally sourced ingredients. We cut and peel local fruit in-house — like our seasonal mangos and pink guavas.
  • In-house pasteurized dairy. Our creamy pops start with milk we pasteurize ourselves for real, clean flavor.
  • Small daily batches. No preservatives, no GMOs, no shortcuts — just popsicles made fresh, the slow way.
About Us

Where architecture meets art & great food.

Cielito began in 2017 when our founder set out to combine her background in architecture with her love of art and great food — and the humble paleta became the perfect canvas.

Every pop is designed like a small piece of architecture: structured, intentional and beautiful. Handcrafted daily in Wynwood with locally sourced fruit and in-house pasteurized dairy.
